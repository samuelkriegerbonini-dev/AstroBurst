Sample data downloaded on demand by AstroBurst: three HST/WFPC2 narrowband frames of the Eagle Nebula (M16)

Files: 502nmos.fits ([OIII] 502 nm), 656nmos.fits (H-alpha 656 nm), 673nmos.fits ([SII] 673 nm)

The frames are not shipped with the installers. The Download sample data entry downloads them on demand from the AstroBurst repository at commit 360a861 (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/samuelkriegerbonini-dev/AstroBurst/360a86196b9316e3cda5c610bc4e6be7225e0988/exampleFits/sample-data/), checks each against its published SHA-256 and keeps them in the data folder's samples/hst-m16-wfpc2/.
