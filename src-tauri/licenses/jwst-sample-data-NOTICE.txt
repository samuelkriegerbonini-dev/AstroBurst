Sample data downloaded on demand by AstroBurst: two JWST sets from programmes 1266 and 2072

Files

- JWST/NIRSpec IFU cube and 1-D spectrum of the Galactic Center (GC-NIRSPEC-1, G395H/F290LP), GTO programme 1266 (PI M. Garcia Marin): jw01266-o005_t001_nirspec_g395h-f290lp_s3d.fits and jw01266-o005_t001_nirspec_g395h-f290lp_x1d.fits, kept in the data folder's samples/jwst-gc-nirspec-ifu/.
- JWST/MIRI LRS slit spectroscopy of the Type Ia supernova SN 2021aefx, GO programme 2072 (PI S. W. Jha): jw02072-o001_t010_miri_p750l_x1d.fits, jw02072-o001_t010_miri_p750l_s2d.fits and jw02072001001_06101_00001_mirimage_cal.fits, kept in the data folder's samples/jwst-miri-lrs/.

The files are not shipped with the installers. The Download sample data entry downloads them on demand from the data-v1 release of the AstroBurst repository (https://github.com/samuelkriegerbonini-dev/AstroBurst/releases/tag/data-v1), checks each against its published SHA-256 and keeps them in the folders named above. They are JWST pipeline products retrieved from the Mikulski Archive for Space Telescopes (MAST) at the Space Telescope Science Institute (STScI), https://mast.stsci.edu/.

Credit: NASA, ESA, CSA. Data obtained from MAST at STScI.

Terms: MAST's data use page (https://archive.stsci.edu/publishing/data-use) states that most data hosted at MAST are in the public domain and have no restrictions on use, and that researchers are expected to acknowledge the originating mission and, usually, STScI in scholarly publications.
