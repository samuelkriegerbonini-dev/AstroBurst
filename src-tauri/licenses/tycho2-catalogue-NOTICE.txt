Data bundled with AstroBurst: catalogs/tycho2-l6-v1.abcat, a star catalogue built from the Tycho-2 Catalogue

What it is

Every installer and the Linux tarball carry catalogs/tycho2-l6-v1.abcat (15541742 bytes, SHA-256 bf01b058e7c9babe03d38d367c98d6a0dbb0e9785741a9c842d053574fa17076), the tier 0 of the offline plate solver. It holds the positions, VT magnitudes and BT-VT colours of the 2,557,501 stars of the Tycho-2 main catalogue and its supplement 1, moved to epoch 2026.0 with their proper motions where they have one, in AstroBurst's compact record type of the Siril HEALPix catalogue format. catalogs/tycho2-l6-v1.PROVENANCE.md, shipped next to it, lists the input files with their SHA-256 and every processing step.

Source

The Tycho-2 Catalogue of the 2.5 Million Brightest Stars: Hog E., Fabricius C., Makarov V.V., Urban S., Corbin T., Wycoff G., Bastian U., Schwekendiek P., Wicenec A., Astron. Astrophys. 355, L27 (2000). CDS catalogue I/259, https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/ftp/I/259/. The catalogue is based on observations by the star mapper of the ESA Hipparcos satellite.
